Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.11.1 (Audit fixes, one set of sizes, and new circles)
Build date: 2026-11-09
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: every "defect" and "design system" item of the 2026-11-09 UI audit (N1-N12, S1-S7, the console and
spell-card items, the Virtues and Flaws panels), and redrawn art (seal, Play crest, roll circle; Modern and Retro).
No rule or content change; character and saga save formats unchanged. Numbered 4.9.11.1 at Haik's request; it
carries visible changes as well as fixes. The audit's older open items (single Play tab row, phone bottom-sheet
console, sticky hero, sourcebooks as a saga setting, Inventory as the armor source, Calculator to Play) are not in
this release.

DECISIONS (Haik, 2026-11-09)
- Odds badges: a caption with a control (not removal).
- Field style: single-line fields underlined, notes boxed, in Build and the Storyguide.
- Scope: defects and design items now; the Play and Build restructuring later.
Our own choices, to confirm: the type scale keeps 14 px (eight steps, 12/13/14/15/16/18/21/27, not the seven the
audit sketched), tablet widths (760-980 px) keep the console beside the Play page, and gold is the only filled
main-action colour ("Make card" and the Storyguide's small secondary actions became gold outlines).

ART (patches/v4.9.11.1/seal.py, seal.css, art.js, art.css)
- Seal: a 120-unit drawing, generated by seal.py: a double rim with fifteen marks (five lozenges, ten dots), two
  pentagons with the fine decagram and a node on each of the ten points, a rayed inner ring, and the eye. Three
  turning layers (.turn, .turn-mid new, .turn-in). Small seals (header, Storyguide bar, chooser) show only the bold
  lines; the main-menu seal shows the fine work. It replaces the 40-unit seal in the header (the menu and the
  Storyguide clone it), in the first-run chooser's script, and in favicon.svg.
- seal.css is placed before the header: the Retro pixel renderer draws the seal once, as soon as it exists, so its
  rules have to be parsed by then (a first build with the rules at the end of the page drew the fine work as noise).
- Retro: bold lines only, one pentagon, no hairlines; the renderer's layer lists gain .turn-mid and .fx-r5 (three
  counted replacements).
- Play crest: the same rim and pentagons around the House mark (crest() is wrapped; its two ring groups are
  replaced).
- Roll circle (fxCircleSvg0 replaced; class names kept, so die, realm, Art and botch tints still apply): double rim
  with five lozenges, the Art names fitted exactly once round the ring (the old text ran past the end of its path),
  a 60-tick ring, ten Form medallions holding the app's own Art icons, a decagram, a pentagon with five Technique
  medallions, and a rayed inner ring (fx-r5, new). The medallions of the Technique and Form being rolled light up
  (from the overlay's data-fx and data-fxt). Retro shows the medallions as studs; lit studs are bright.

AUDIT DEFECTS (fix.css, fix.js, edits.py)

  line; cards at 600 px and below. Table roles are restored in script. Was: 790 px of table in a 648 px card at a
  1280 px window.
- N2 Six notes no longer cite section numerals (two were wrong since 4.9.7.6 renumbered the sheet).
- N3 Settings: the Done row is sticky at the bottom of the dialog; fieldsets are 20 px apart.
- N4 Roll ceremony: "Tap to skip to the result" / "Tap to continue" on touch screens; veil .86 -> .95.
- N5 Odds: one caption under the Characteristic tiles with - and + for the Ease Factor (steps of 3) and, where a
  tab has opposed totals, the opposing total. It drives P().ef and P().oppTotal, which the badges already used.
- N6 641-900 px: the header's subtitle shortens to the version so the header stays on one row. 760-980 px: Play
  keeps two columns with the compact (phone) header block.
- N7 Phone Combat: one card per weapon; action rows wrap inside the card.
- N8 A rail jump subtracts the sticky summary strip's height.
- N9 Storyguide headings focused by script show no outline.
- N10 The console's own sound button is hidden; the header's (File menu on a phone) remains.
- N11 "Sound on"/"Sound off", "Main menu"; one Storyguide tile description before and after its code loads.
- N12 Retro light: credits and settings icons readable; Characteristic names not truncated on phones; the Aura
  select is 32 px tall on touch; section chips wrap on desktop instead of fading out mid-word.

DESIGN ITEMS
- S1 Type scale (scale.py, applied to every <style> block at patch time): 175 declarations snapped. Sizes in use on
  the Build sheet 16 -> 10, on Play 15 -> 9; no running text under 12 px. Tokens --fs-1..8 for new rules. Sizes set
  from scripts, in em, or above 27 px are untouched.
- S2 Radii: 107 single-value declarations snapped to 4 / 8 / 12 px (tokens --r-1..3). Button heights: 28 inline,
  34 header and default, 42 primary (tokens --bh-s/m/l); tabs and tiles are not buttons and keep their sizes.
- S3 Fields: Build's borderless inline fields outside tables get a faint underline; Storyguide single-line fields
  are underlined; notes stay boxed.
- S4 Whole-pixel hairlines: the page's 1.45 line height produced 21.75, 23.2, 18.85 and 17.4 px lines; the common
  rows and fields now have whole-pixel line heights. 1 px borders on fractional pixels, Modern: Build 205 -> 63
  (1280 px), 196 -> 25 (390 px); Play 98 -> 32, 72 -> 32. Retro Build 107 -> 25, Play 32 -> 9. Not zero.
- S5 .btn.primary is gold (--gilt, text --on-gilt); blue stays for links and selection.
- S6 Play header: wounds grid, Confidence "Score"/"Points", Burden only when above 0, figures on a grid on phones.
- S7 The roll's total is set at 40 px.
- Console: Reset is hidden while the play state equals its defaults (navigation, Ease Factor and opposing total
  aside); the aura fold is hidden on the empty console. Spell cards: R/D/T line and a bar key.
- Build: Virtues and Flaws panels no longer stretch to equal height.
