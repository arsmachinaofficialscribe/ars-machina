Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.11.4 (Odds controls moved to Modifiers)
Build date: 2026-11-10
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: one Play change at Haik's direction. No rule or content change. Play state gains an optional `odds`
object; character files without it load as before.

- Removed: the 4.9.11.1 odds caption under the Characteristic tiles (div.f9111-odds), its script and its styles.
  Its controls wrote P().ef and P().oppTotal, which are also the Ease Factor and opposing total of the next roll,
  so a player could change a roll without meaning to.
- Added: Modifiers › "Odds badges" in the roll console (consoleMods wrapped): a note saying what the % badges are, a
  tick box "Measure the badges against my own numbers" (off by default), and, when ticked, two number fields.
  State: P().odds = {on, ef, opp}; ef 0..60, opp -30..99, rounded and clamped when read.
- oddsChip is wrapped. Only when odds.on is true, and only for a check that takes the page's Ease Factor
  (label "Ease Factor", value equal to P().ef) or the page's opposing total (label naming an opponent, value equal
  to P().oppTotal), the badge is computed against odds.ef or odds.opp. Checks with an Ease Factor of their own
  (from a rule or a table) and opposed checks with a rolled opponent are untouched. No roll reads P().odds.
- "Reset manual modifiers" shows while the box is ticked and clears it, like any other modifier.
- Known gap, by this decision: on a touch screen, with no tooltip, the page itself no longer says what the badges
  are measured against; the note in Modifiers does.
