## 4.9.7.0: Realms of Power: The Infernal

**Sin (pp. 20–22).**
- There are two grades of sin: mortal and venial.
- Sin needs a conscious act. A character compelled by a supernatural power does not sin.
- Dealing with demons, or using Infernal powers (even unknowingly), stains the soul each time.
- True repentance removes the mark of sin.

**Sinful traits (p. 21).** Each sin is opposed by a virtue. A virtuous trait reads as the sin at a negative score, so Dedicated +3 (Loyal) is Proud –3.

| Sin | Its traits | Opposing virtue | Its traits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proud | Arrogant, Haughty, Rebellious, Suspicious, Vain | Loyal | Dedicated, Faithful, Humble, Reliable, Trusting |
| Wrathful | Angry, Desperate, Spiteful, Vengeful, Violent | Calm | Cheerful, Hopeful, Optimistic, Patient, Peaceful |
| Envious | Cruel, Gossipy, Gruff, Jealous, Meddlesome | Kind | Charitable, Compassionate, Forgiving, Generous, Merciful |
| Slothful | Cowardly, Cynical, Indecisive, Lazy, Shy | Brave | Bold, Courageous, Determined, Ready, Zealous |
| Lustful | Fanatical, Lecherous, Naïve, Overconfident, Reckless | Wise | Careful, Cautious, Chaste, Practical, Prudent |
| Greedy | Gluttonous, Needy, Self-Indulgent, Sickly, Weak | Strong | Healthy, Pious, Steady, Temperate, Vigilant |
| Avaricious | Ambitious, Corrupt, Cunning, Devious, Manipulative | Just | Even-handed, Fair, Honest, Honorable, Straightforward |

- **(ours)** Personality Flaws are mapped to sins by name: Lecherous → Lustful; Cowardly and Lazy → Slothful; Vain → Proud; Cruel → Envious.

**Personality Flaws as traits (p. 22).** A Minor Personality Flaw counts as a +3 trait and a Major one as +6.

**Infernal influence (p. 23).**
- Roll simple die + Presence + Leadership.
- The trait gained follows the chart: 9 → 1, 12 → 2, 15 → 3, 18 → 4, 21 → 5, 24 → 6 (the maximum).
- Personal influence takes a season. Ceremonial influence costs a Confidence Point and needs Infernal Lore 1.
- Only an Inspirational character can influence strangers ceremonially. A character who resists is not affected.
- Traits from personal influence fade after about a year; from ceremonial influence, after about a week.

**Confidence through sin (p. 24).**
- Two ways, each needing a trait that shows the sin:
  - indulging the sin for a season gives a Confidence Point;
  - a ritual of the sin gives a Confidence Point and an infernal Warping Point.
- Venial sins are enough for traits of +3 or less. Above that it usually takes a mortal sin.
- A season in a tarnished aura usually earns a Confidence Point.
- Characters without a Confidence Score still gain these points. They may spend them on ceremonial influence, on tarnishing, or at +1 each on a roll of that trait, up to the trait's score.

**Infernal Warping (pp. 17–18).**
- Effects by Warping Score:
  - WS 1: a sinful Minor Personality Flaw (Delusion, Obsessed, Compulsion, or Weak-Willed).
  - WS 3: Offensive to Animals if the Warping comes mostly from venial sins; Tainted with Evil if from mortal sins or Infernal traffic.
  - WS 5: a Minor Supernatural Virtue (Enticer of Multitudes, Infernal Protection, Gender Shift, Paragon Through Sin, or Infernal Blessings (Minor)).
  - WS 6+: Age Quickly, Enfeebled, or Plagued by a Demon.
- Sources:
  - a ritual of sin;
  - strong Infernal auras, except for characters with Infernal powers or Abilities, who are immune to them;
  - Infernal powers of level 30 or more, or costing 6 or more Might;
  - each zero on a botch die with an unholy power.

**Infernalists (pp. 94–96).**
- **Hierarchy Score** = the character's highest Infernal Reputation.
  - A character may start with an Infernal Reputation of 1 if he has Infernal or tainted traits.
  - Infernal Reputations cannot come from a Flaw that grants a bad Reputation.
- **Vituperation:**
  - Triggered by gaining 2 or more Warping Points at once, or by failing to control Infernal power.
  - Roll Sta + Concentration + Confidence Score + stress die vs. Warping Score + points gained + Hierarchy + aura + stress die (no botch).
  - On a failure, it lasts as long as Wizard's Twilight would at that Warping Score (the book cites ArM5 p. 88).
  - Afterwards roll for aging: stress die (no botch) + age / 10 rounded up – living conditions + Warping Score. Longevity Rituals do not apply.
  - The Confidence Score rises by one if it is below the Warping Score or Decrepitude.
  - He gains Confidence Points equal to the Warping Points gained, unless the avoidance roll botched.
  - He may accept Vituperation without rolling.
- **Sacrifice** adds to the effect total: effigy +2, animal +4, black animal +6, human +8, innocent human +10. It takes about an hour more.
  - **(ours)** Applied to the maleficium roll.
- **Profaning:**
  - A Confidence Point turns a holy object into one pawn of vis, usually vis infesta.
  - For a relic, its Faith stands in for the Confidence Point, but the character's Confidence Score must exceed the relic's Faith Score.

**Goetic magic and the False Gift (p. 124).**
- A magus may use Rego for a Goetic Art and Vim for the (Realm) Lore, if he has that Goetic Art.
  - **(ours)** The substitution is made only when it is higher.
- He may bond a spirit as his familiar.
- Goetic Mastery adds a Goetic Art to the Casting Total, and Hierarchy × 5 to Penetration against demons.
- The False Gift: a character undergoes Vituperation instead of Twilight. Absolution removes the Gift.

**Corrupted beasts (p. 78).**

| Entry | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Greater Infernal Power | Major Supernatural Virtue | +5 Infernal Might; a power that needs Penetration, level up to Might + 10 |
| Lesser Infernal Power | Minor Supernatural Virtue | +2 Infernal Might; a power without Penetration, level up to Might |
| Horrifying Appearance | Major Supernatural Flaw | — |

- All three are for corrupted beasts only.
- **(ours)** A saved "Infernal Power" Virtue becomes Lesser Infernal Power, since both are Minor.

## 4.9.7.0: Realms of Power: The Divine

**Schism (p. 41).** Two leaders with authority can each invoke a different temper in the same aura. Both apply, so a bonus and a penalty may cancel.

**Relics (pp. 43–45).**
- A relic holds Faith Points equal to its Faith Score. They give Magic Resistance, or are spent like Confidence.
- Divine Might = Faith × 10, refreshed each sunrise.
- **Scourging:** Infernal beings whose Might does not exceed the relic's cannot come within its Might in feet. Those that do take damage equal to its Might each round.
- Its powers work only for its faith, and fail while the bearer is impious.
- For a ruler, the most powerful relic's Magic Resistance adds to the commanding aura's, but not to its Soak.
  - **(ours)** Otherwise the higher Magic Resistance applies (DE p. 55).

**Tragedy of hubris (p. 46).**
- The tragedy depends on how far the total fell short:

| Shortfall | Tragedy | Possible results |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Minor | An Ability halved; a Minor Flaw; a Light Wound; three extra botch dice; or a Warping Point |
| 11–20 | Major | An Ability lost; a Major Flaw; a Heavy Wound; a permanent minor tragedy; or a Decrepitude Point |
| 21+ | Ultimate | All Supernatural Abilities lost; possession; an Incapacitating Wound; a permanent major tragedy; or a regional calamity |

- With True Faith, Faith × 10 stands in for the total.

**Divine Warping (p. 62).**
- No Warping from strong Divine auras for characters with holy powers, Divine Virtues or Faith Points.
- Minor Virtues gained at Warping Score 5 stop further Warping from auras.
- Witnesses of a miracle of level 30 or more gain Warping; its beneficiaries do not. Holy powers of level 30 or more also cause Warping.
- Each zero on a botch die gives a Warping Point.
- The book lists the common Minor and Major Flaws of Divine Warping.

**Divine Ascent (pp. 63–65).**
- **Who:** holy characters with a holy Power or Miraculous Ability. Characters with only True Faith take ordinary Warping. Hermetic magi with True Faith take Twilight.
- **Ascension roll:** Presence + Concentration (or Meditation) + stress die vs. Warping Score + points gained + True Faith + aura + stress die (no botch).
  - On a failure he gains extra Warping Points equal to the margin.
  - The Ascent lasts (Warping Score + points gained – Faith) days, at least 1. From Warping Score 6 it lasts that many months.
  - **(ours)** The rule box says Presence. The book's example uses Intelligence and adds up one higher than its dice.
- **Divine Gloom:** each Warping Point gained gives an XP to holy Abilities and takes one from mundane Abilities.
- **Divine Form:**
  - Magic Resistance = Warping Score + True Faith × 10.
  - Soak = that total ÷ 5.
  - Penetration = Warping Score + Faith × 5.
  - An impious act rolls a stress die with 3 × Warping Score more botch dice.
  - A venial sin gives a Light Wound; a mortal sin a Medium Wound.
- **Divine Unity:** the Warping Score is added to the personified virtue's trait. Others are influenced toward it, and auras take its temper.

**Saints (pp. 87–89).**
- **Invoke:** Com + Charm + modifiers + simple die vs. 15 + simple die, once a day.
  - Modifiers: tomb +1; Divine aura +aura; feast day +3; pilgrimage +2; touching a relic +1; donation +1 to +3; imminent peril +2; True Faith +Faith; unchristian act –1 to –3; no donation within a year –3; a miracle within a month –20; not the patron saint –15.
  - **(ours)** The book has the storyguide roll the saint's die in secret; the app rolls it openly.
- **Threaten:** Pre + Leadership + modifiers + stress die vs. 9 + Divine Might ÷ 5.
  - Modifiers: bones +1; whipping +2; berating +1; barring veneration +3; peril +1; a miracle within a month –20; an unfamiliar saint –15.
  - Confidence may be spent on this roll; Faith may not.
  - A failure means no petitions answered for a month. 10 or more short brings a minor curse and no aid for a season. A botch brings the saint's curse.
- **Saints' Divine Might:** the Virgin Mary 100, greater saints 75, area saints 50, local saints 25.
- **Penetration** of a saint's power = Divine Might – 5 × its cost.

**Golems (pp. 142–143).**
- **Awaken:** Int + Kabbalah + Wonders + Ceremony bonus + Dominion aura against 30 each season. Points over 30 accumulate.
  - In the final season, Might = accumulated points + a stress die, capped at Kabbalah × 5.
  - There is one botch die per kabbalist.
- **Empower:** the same total against the power's level. Points over the level accumulate until they equal the level.
  - A golem holds magnitudes of powers up to its Might.
- **Madness roll:** botch → madness; 0–4 → Trusting –1; 5–9 → Vengeful +1; 10–14 → Loyal –1; 15+ → roll twice more.
  - **(ours)** Applied as printed.

## Reading for the rules notes (written at release)

- The half-aura line written at v1.4.3 ("rounded up") is replaced: where no rounding is stated, round down (DE p. 6). This covers half a Faerie aura for Hermetic magic (DE p. 411) and for holy magic (RoP:D p. 69). The app has done this since 4.8.5.0.
