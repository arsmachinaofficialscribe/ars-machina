Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.8.0 (Lighter start, smoother changes)
Build date: 2026-10-28
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: loading and rendering only. No rule, data or save-format change.

Network payload at start-up (hosted build)
- The idle-time loop in the site-prefetch script that fetched every die's hero and tumble pictures (30 dice, about
  2 MB) is removed. The die-face engraver (v4.8.7.0) no longer preloads every die's hero picture in idle time.
- window.amWarmDie(key) warms one die (Modern: tumble + hero; Retro: tumble + resting sprite) and the engraver's
  picture for it. After each Play draw, in idle time, the die of the selected check (getCheck(P().sel), dieArtKey)
  is warmed, so the first roll still has its frames.
- The bestiary and hedge lists (am49-data, 1.9 MB raw) are no longer fetched in idle time after load. They load
  when a panel asks (unchanged getter, v4.9.0.0) or when the pointer or focus reaches the Realms, Magic or Hedge
  tab or the Storyguide menu item.
- The spell catalogue (v33-catalog-data) ships as plain JSON in the hosted build (pack.py PLAIN_JSON); the server
  compresses it. The offline file keeps the zlib block.
- Measured in Chromium, served with brotli, before entering the app: 82 requests, 3,403,208 bytes (v4.9.7.6) →
  15 requests, 805,291 bytes.

Animations and page changes
- applyTheme, applySkin and applyFonts first hold every Build section and Play section lying more than 0.6 of a
  screen outside the viewport (class am-cv: content-visibility auto at its measured size). They are released one
  per idle callback from 0.7 s later. Page length and scroll position do not change; rail jumps land as before.
- The sound button's overlap check (v4.8.4.0) runs 140 ms after scrolling stops instead of on every scrolled frame,
  and its change watcher waits for 300 ms of quiet instead of firing every 300 ms during an animation.
- Retro pixel sigils (retro-skin script) are not redrawn while the menu or a roll overlay covers them
  (window.amTopLayer).
- The roll overlay (.fx) has no backdrop blur. Its scrim is 86 % opaque; screenshots before and after show no
  visible difference.
- Measured at 4x CPU slowdown, 1350 px wide, mean frame time during the change: theme 147 ms → 32 ms, theme back
  91 ms → 30 ms; skin about 100 ms → 48–64 ms (noisy). At 390 px: theme 90 ms → 19 ms, skin 83 ms → 31 ms.
  Script time during a 1.2 s smooth scroll of the Build sheet: 380 ms → 66–81 ms (390 px: 500 ms → 115 ms).
  Menu pages, folds and the roll were already at 60 frames per second and are unchanged.

Main-thread work at load
- The first-run chooser no longer forces a layout of the half-parsed page while html[data-booting] is set; its
  ResizeObserver positions the previews after the first layout. Its palette lookup reads the style sheets once per
  sheet count and keeps only :root rules.
- No inflate of the spell catalogue in the hosted build (its script: about 100 ms → 25 ms at 4x).
- Measured at 4x CPU slowdown from navigation to 4 s after load (Lighthouse-style grouping):
  1350 px: 2.65–2.76 s → 2.0–2.1 s of main-thread work; time over 50 ms per task 1.16–1.21 s → 0.68–0.79 s.
  390 px: 2.94 s → 2.28 s; 1.42 s → 1.05 s.
- Not changed: the boot sheet draw (renderAll, about 85 ms at full speed) and the first layout; the start-up sorts
  with Intl.Collator; script compile of the inline code.

Tests
- tests/run-tests.js: new perfChecks block (no die art or bestiary file at start-up; at most 20 start-up requests;
  plain JSON catalogue loads; the selected check's die is warmed and no other; pointing at Realms fetches the lists;
  a theme change holds off-screen sections and restores them all; rail jumps land at one offset; scrolling runs at
  most 10 hit tests). The static check reads plain or zlib JSON blocks.
