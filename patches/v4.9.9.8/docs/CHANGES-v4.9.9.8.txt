Version v4.9.9.8 (Sample companions and grogs)
Build date: 2026-10-28
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: New Character content. No rule values changed; save format unchanged. Numbered as a patch, following the
author's recent releases.

New Character › Start from
- Magus: a blank sheet or the example (unchanged).
- Companion: a blank sheet, The knight (DE p. 34) or The female scholar (DE p. 33).
- Grog: a blank sheet, The hunter (DE p. 32) or The standard soldier (DE p. 32).
  The "The example" choice no longer appears for companions and grogs (it always opened a magus). Switching the
  kind of character resets an invalid choice to a blank sheet.

The samples are the Definitive Edition's character templates as printed: Characteristics, age, Virtues and
Flaws (with their details), Abilities with specialties, Personality Traits, Reputations, weapons and armor, and
the template's customization notes (in Persona › Notes). Ability experience is each score's cost, assigned to
the creation stages: early childhood (45 XP, childhood Abilities only), later life (15 a year, 20 with Wealthy),
and Warrior's 50 bonus experience (DE pp. 48–49). The totals match the book's budgets exactly:
knight 45 + 400, scholar 45 + 225, hunter 45 + 225 + 50, soldier 45 + 300 + 50.
- Left as the book leaves them: the native language's name, character names (each opens titled by its template),
  the standard soldier's Weakness ("can be chosen freely", DE p. 32).
- The knight's Relic Virtue adds its relic (True Faith 1) to the Inventory as for any sheet with that Virtue.
- Each sample passes all of the sheet's creation checks (tested).
