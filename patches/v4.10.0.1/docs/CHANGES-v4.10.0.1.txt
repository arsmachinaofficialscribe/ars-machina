FIX — the cursor jumped to the start of a field while typing ("25" became "52")
- Cause: a keystroke in some fields redraws the part of the page that holds them. Build: a field that changes the
  experience pools redraws its section through the 4.9.10.0 keyed redraw (window.amWorkSet), e.g. Years since
  Gauntlet. Play: every keystroke redraws Play through the 4.9.11.0 keyed redraw (amPlaySet); a field inside a block
  that mixes words with elements is rebuilt whole. The rebuilt field was then refocused (renderAll by id, renderPlay by
  data-pk) with the caret at the start; number fields have no setSelectionRange, so the next digit went in front.
- Fix: before the redraw, the field being typed in is kept; afterwards it is put back in place of its rebuilt copy
  (same data-k or data-pk, same kind of field), taking the new markup's attributes, so it keeps its caret and
  selection. Its value is replaced only when the sheet's value differs (a clamped number). renderPlay no longer moves
  the caret of a kept text field to its end.
- Code: Play in the 4.9.11.0 script (typing, putBack in amPlaySet's set); Build in the new v4.10.0.1 script (an
  amWorkSet wrapper).
