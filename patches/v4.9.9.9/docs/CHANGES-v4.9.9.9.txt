Scope: fixes only (D1–D8 of the 2026-10-29 UI audit). No rule or content change; save format unchanged.

D1  Phone first visit: the chooser's controls row wrapped while the fallback font was in use and unwrapped when the
    web font arrived (layout shift 0.275 at 390 px). Between 360 and 560 px the three controls now sit in a
    three-column grid that cannot wrap; the rule lives in the chooser's own style block so it applies before paint.
    Measured: 0.0057 at 390 px, 0.0043 at 360 px.
D2  Phone touch targets: fatigue pips keep their 15 px look with a 25 × 32 px hit area (gap 12 px); wound +/− are
    32 px. Applies with a coarse pointer or below 600 px.
D3  Field names: fields in tables without a label take one from their column header and row (Ability free XP,
    Art XP and vis, raw vis pawns: 53 fields on the example), applied after every redraw.
D4  Section headers no longer carry role="button", tabindex or aria-expanded; the header's Fold or Start button
    folds it. Clicking the header still folds it.
D5  The aura cell shows its state with data-on instead of aria-pressed on a span; the console's tabs sit in their own
    role="tablist" (the speaker button is outside it), with roving tabindex and Left/Right/Home/End keys.
D6  Contrast (all now 4.5:1 or better in Modern and Retro, dark and light, Build and Play):
    Modern light --gilt #99731d → #7d5d14, --muted #59656f → #505b64; Retro light --muted #6b5334 → #3f2e18,
    --gilt #8a5a0c → #734a09, --good #2e6a1e → #285c1a; optional rail steps 62% → 90% opacity; folded optional
    sections 72% → 90%, their seal no longer greyed; casting-grid numbers white (dark) or near-black (light);
    the pinned chip and the light status pill use the muted and good colours.
D7  The global speaker button moves into the header tools beside Credits and Settings (desktop) or the File menu
    (phone) while a character is open; over the main menu it floats in the bottom corner as before. The Settings note
    says so.
D8  Settings › Starting up: "under the ☰ button" → "select the seal at the top left".
