Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.7.0 (rules: sin and sanctity, from Realms of Power: The Infernal and The Divine)
Build date: 2026-10-27
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Realms tab
- Shown for every character while any Realms of Power book is switched on in Build's sources, and hidden when all four
  are off, like other sourcebook sections. Its sections follow their books: sin and infernalists need The Infernal;
  saints, relics, the Divine Ascent and golems need The Divine.
- Sin and temptation (RoP:I pp. 20-22): each Personality Trait can be tied to one of the seven sins or its opposing
  virtue, guessed from the trait's name (the p. 21 table) or chosen; a virtuous trait reads as the sin at a negative
  score. Personality Flaws named for a sin count as +3 (Minor) or +6 (Major) (p. 22). Counts of venial and mortal sins
  since absolution, cleared by absolution.
- Confidence through sin (RoP:I p. 24): a season of indulgence gives a Confidence Point; a ritual of the sin gives a
  Confidence Point and an infernal Warping Point (p. 18).
- Infernal influence (RoP:I p. 23): simple die + Presence + Leadership; 9/12/15/18/21/24 give a trait of 1 to 6.
  Ceremonial influence needs Infernal Lore 1 and a Confidence Point.
- Warping by realm: Infernal Warping effects at Warping Scores 1, 3, 5 and 6+ and their sources (RoP:I pp. 17-18);
  Divine Warping's common Flaws, immunities and sources (RoP:D p. 62).
- Saints (RoP:D pp. 87-89): invoke a saint, Communication + Charm + modifiers + simple die vs 15 + simple die, with
  the eleven modifiers; threaten a saint, Presence + Leadership + modifiers + stress die vs 9 + Divine Might / 5, with
  the seven modifiers and the month, season and curse results; Confidence may be spent on a threat, Faith Points may
  not. Saints from the bestiary, or the four categories (Divine Might 100/75/50/25). The miracles and curses list with
  each power's Penetration (Divine Might - 5 x cost).
- Relics (RoP:D pp. 43-45): for relics in Inventory, Faith Points with a sunrise refresh, Divine Might (Faith x 10),
  scourging the Infernal, Magic Resistance of the most powerful relic.

Infernalists (RoP:I pp. 94-96, 124)
- Hierarchy Score = the highest Infernal Reputation.
- Vituperation: Stamina + Concentration + Confidence Score + stress die vs Warping Score + points gained + Hierarchy
  + aura + stress die (no botch); the Wizard's Twilight table for its length; the aging roll that follows (stress die
  no botch + age / 10 rounded up - living conditions + Warping Score); Confidence Score and Points afterwards; the
  option to accept it unrolled.
- Sacrifice adds +2/+4/+6/+8/+10 to a maleficium's total.
- Profaning: a Confidence Point turns a holy object into a pawn of vis infesta (added under Raw vis); relic rules noted.
- Goetic magic: a magus may use Rego for a Goetic Art and Vim for the Lore; Goetic Mastery noted. The False Gift:
  Twilight is replaced by Vituperation.

Holy characters (RoP:D pp. 41, 46, 63-65, 142-143)
- A failed holy power names the tragedy of hubris it risks (minor 1-10 short, major 11-20, ultimate 21+), with True
  Faith x 10 standing in for the total.
- The Divine Ascent: Ascension roll (Presence + Concentration or Meditation + stress die vs Warping Score + points
  gained + True Faith + aura + stress die), extra Warping Points equal to the margin, the Ascent's length, and the
  effects of Divine Gloom, Divine Form (Magic Resistance, Soak, Penetration, impious botch dice as a roll option) and
  Divine Unity.
- Schism: a rival temper in a Dominion aura's settings, applied as a second temper.
- Golems: Awaken Golem Total (Intelligence + Kabbalah + Wonders + Ceremony bonus + Dominion aura) against 30 each
  season, the final stress die and the Kabbalah x 5 cap; Empower Golem Total against a power's level; the madness roll.

Corrupted beasts (RoP:I p. 78)
- Greater Infernal Power (Major) and Lesser Infernal Power (Minor) replace the unsourced "Infernal Power" Virtue;
  Horrifying Appearance (Major Flaw) added; a build check that they need the Corrupted Beast Flaw. A saved "Infernal
  Power" Virtue opens as Lesser Infernal Power.

Dice
- The new rolls use the realm dice: Divine for Ascension, saints and golems; Infernal for influence and Vituperation.

Optional rules (Settings, new "Optional rules" tab beside "General")
- Two lists. Sub rules: optional or variant rules printed in the books, and the author's rulings. Homebrew rules:
  readings where the books are silent or unclear. Defaults follow the Definitive Edition first, then the author's
  rulings, then the recommended readings. Choices are kept in this browser; "Reset optional rules" restores them.
- Sub rules: the storyguide rolls the saint's die in secret (on; RoP:D p. 87); Savantism's full-rate option is offered
  (on; author's ruling; DE p. 143); holy magi cast on the Divine die (on; author's ruling).
- Homebrew rules (recommended setting in brackets): Faerie Magic keeps foreign-aura botch dice in a Faerie aura (on;
  DE pp. 236, 411); holy magic keeps foreign-aura botch dice (on; RoP:D p. 69); assume about 2 Warping Points a year
  after Gauntlet when none are recorded (on; DE p. 32); Hope doubles extra Faith Points on a miracle (off; RoP:D pp. 60,
  62); the Ars Notoria pool also buys Artes Liberales (off; p. 92); Divine Ascension uses Presence, not Intelligence
  (on; p. 63); a relic's Magic Resistance adds to other Magic Resistance (off; RoP:D p. 43, DE p. 55); Goetic magic
  substitutes Rego and Vim only where higher (on; RoP:I p. 124); sacrifice also adds to Goetic Art totals (off; p. 95).
