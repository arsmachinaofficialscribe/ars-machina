Scope: numbered as a patch at the author's request; it adds saints and relic content. Save format unchanged; new

Play › Realms › Saints
- The saint list adds the 25 saints of "More Saints" (RoP:D p. 91) with Divine Might by category (greater 75, area
  50, local 25) and patronage. The book gives them no powers. St Patrick, St Kevin and St John the Evangelist are not
  added again: they are statted as Saint Patrick (CI p. 134), Saint Kevin of Glendalough (CI p. 135) and
  St. John the Apostle (Antagonists p. 61).
- The chosen saint's powers are listed with each power's cost and Penetration (Divine Might – 5 × cost, RoP:D p. 88).
- Your patron saint: invoking that saint counts as invoking your patron; any other saint takes –15 (RoP:D p. 87).
- Your saints: name, kind (greater, area or local, RoP:D p. 89), patronage, and powers chosen from the book's
  miracles (p. 88) and curses (p. 89), or taken from a sample saint (p. 89). The total cost is shown against the
  rule of thumb, a fifth of the Divine Might (p. 89), with the category guidance (p. 88). A saint from the p. 91 list
  can be made one of your saints to give it powers. Basis for custom and local saints: DE p. 501; RoP:D p. 89.
- A patron or a saint of your own keeps the Saints section on the Divine sub-tab.

Play › Realms › Relics
- Each relic: highest Faith Score in any faith (Divine Might = that × 10; DE p. 419; RoP:D p. 45), the saint it
  belongs to, and its powers, taken from that saint or chosen (DE p. 419; RoP:D p. 45). Using a power spends its cost
  from the relic's Divine Might until sunrise; Penetration is the relic's Divine Might – 5 × cost (our reading,
  following the saints' rule, RoP:D p. 88).
- Sample relics (RoP:D p. 44): the Head of John the Baptist (Faith 5, Might 50; Resist Temptation, The Faithful Made
  Whole), Nehushtan (Faith 3 Jewish / 2 Christian or Muslim, Might 30; Mass Healing), the Ark of the Covenant
  (Faith 10 Jewish / 7 Christian or Muslim, Might 100; powers at the storyguide's choice).
- The note adds the DE rule: one relic's benefits at a time; relying on more than one is a sin and loses all (DE p. 419).
