LAYOUT (patches/v4.9.10.0/sheet.css, sheet.js)
- Section cards stay; inside them, lists sit in boxes (sunk panel, rule border, header band) and rows are divided
  by hairlines: Virtue and Flaw columns, Personality Traits and Reputations, every table in a section (Characteristics,
  Abilities, Arts, weapons), the spell and possession lists, and Personality's sub-groups (Warping, Aging and Twilight;
  Longevity Ritual; Raw Vis), which are wrapped in .f910-box after each redraw. Biography and Combat sub-groups too.
- Spells: one grid row (name, Arts, Level, Casting, Max learnable, details, remove), column names on the first row
  only (the others keep them for screen readers). Range, Duration, Target, mastery, notes and the "As in the book"
  line open behind a chevron (button.f910-more, aria-expanded); open rows are kept for the session.
- Possessions: the same, with columns Kind, Number, Kept, Load each, Load and the Ready button.
- Sources: "Ars Magica Definitive Edition p. 79" and every supplement title become a chip "DE p. 79" (codes from
  BOOKS; ArM5 shown as DE), the full title in the tooltip. On a Virtue or Flaw the chip sits on the name line
  (Retro: on the detail line, so names are not cut).
- Free box on a Virtue or Flaw: shown when ticked, or while the row is hovered or focused.
- Fields inside rows and tables have no border until hovered or focused.
- House picker: crests 32 px in a strip of 132 px tiles (two or three rows instead of two rows of 120 px tiles).
- Ledger (audit C4): Experience ring shows "/ total"; Best Art leaves the rings for a text line.
- Retro: the 4 px red stripe painted down the left of every section header (72 % of its height, so the seal cut it
  into two red ticks) is gone. Each section card instead hangs a pixel bookmark ribbon from its top frame: a 9 × 16
  pixel sprite (inline SVG, crisp edges) drawn at 2× (18 × 32 px) in the left gutter, with a 2 px hard drop shadow;
  it is gold while the section is pinned. Retro section cards have 32 px left padding (was 22) so the ribbon clears
  the seal. A folded section's summary chips sit 10 px above the gold rule under the heading (they touched it).
- Phones (≤ 600 px): the section header's pill column flexes and its text wraps, so it never runs under the i,
  pin and Fold buttons (it did on Abilities, Arts and others in 4.9.9.9).
- Desktop example magus: Build page 13,957 → 11,989 px; phone 21,468 → 17,811 px. Spells 1,744 → 673 px,
  Virtues and Flaws 1,341 → 929 px, Inventory 1,692 → 1,148 px.

SPEED (patches/v4.9.10.0/perf.js; phone width, 4x CPU throttling, example magus)
- Typing: the input listener schedules refresh() (window.amRefreshSoon): number fields once a frame, text fields
  280 ms after the last key; focusout, pointerdown, Enter/Tab, pagehide, a hidden page, renderAll and setMode run it
  at once. setPath still stores every key in st immediately. Time to paint a keystroke in Name: 145–200 → 31–68 ms.
- Keyed redraw: renderAll hands the fourteen section strings to window.amWorkSet, which keeps each section whose
  markup is unchanged (combobox and portrait-clip counters masked) and replaces only the others; anything unexpected
  falls back to innerHTML. The 4.9.7.6 grouping step moves sections only when their order or groups changed.
  Adding a Personality Trait: renderAll 310–335 → 120–145 ms.
- Forced layouts removed: the 4.9.9.9 sound-button check read offsetParent inside every redraw (and in its body
  class observer), forcing a full-page layout mid-redraw (130–216 ms on a phone). It now runs only debounced and
  decides header or File menu from the 640 px breakpoint.
- Laboratory: labRefresh waits while its section is folded and runs when the section opens.
- Not changed, measured: start-up 15 requests, 0.76 MB brotli (0.76 MB in 4.9.9.9); hosted page 2.49 MB
  (0.55 MB brotli); offline file 8.79 MB. The desktop start-up "layout shift 0.11–0.23" in the audit tool is the
  shift after its own click on Enter (hadRecentInput not filtered); a real first visit measures 0.004.
