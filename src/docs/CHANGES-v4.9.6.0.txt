Realm dice
- Three new d10s rendered with the toolkit's own renderer (tools/dice: three.js 0.169 in headless Chromium).
  Its geometry and camera were fitted to the shipped dice's face outlines (DIE_FACE_DATA.geo) to within 0.00002, and a
  test render's silhouette overlaps the shipped Hermetic die by 99.9%.
  Divine: alabaster lit from within, gold veins, a six-winged seraph and gold rim.
  Infernal: charred iron with ember cracks, a heraldic dragon's head (ember inlay in a dark groove), smoky red rim.
  Faerie: dragonfly-wing glass in teal, violet and rose with fine veins, a violet dragonfly, pearl rim.
- Each die has a hero image, a 12-frame tumble sheet, Retro hero and tumble sprites, Retro faces 0-9 and "10" with
  matte high-contrast numerals, roll-log chips, face shading data for Modern engraving, a landing sound, and chip and
  numeral colours. 84 asset files, 263 KB, fetched only when a die is first shown.
- Rolls: Divine — holy powers, prayer, holy influence, miracles, God's aid, tempers, Ars Notoria, and a holy magus's
  spellcasting unless "Cast this as Hermetic magic instead" is ticked. Infernal — maleficia, Goetic Arts, Scouring,
  tarnishes. Faerie — faerie rites, Curse-Throwing. Might-based beings' own rolls are mapped to their realm's die; the
  app has none yet.
- Roll circle: Divine — a turning halo of gold rays, six feathered wings unfurling and beating, rising motes.
  Infernal — crimson flames licking around the circle, blood-red embers, the circle tinted blood red.
  Faerie — four-pointed sparks orbiting and twinkling, the circle tinted teal. Transform and opacity animation only, at
  most 30 elements, none with reduced motion; Retro steps the motion, draws with crisp edges and tints its pixel
  circle.

Rules
- Training packages (Grogs pp. 66-73): 7 childhood, 41 career and 10 non-career packages in Build's Early childhood and
  Later life stages, with choices where the book gives them; the suggested age (Wealthy one quarter less, Poor half
  as much again, p. 68); checks for 15 years per package and for the Flaws or Virtue a package needs. Every five-year
  option totals 75 XP and every three-year option 45 (Natural Philosopher's formulae excepted, A&A ch. 5).
- Savantism (Definitive Edition p. 143): v4.8.3.0 already halved the childhood and later-life pools (rounded down),
  capped Abilities at 3 (favoured 6) and halved Seasons Advancement Totals except the favoured Ability's. Added: the
  apprenticeship and after-Gauntlet pools are halved too; the favoured Ability may reach 6 below age 30; an optional
  per-character rule buys the favoured Ability at full rate; a note in Build's Abilities section explains the Flaw.
- Feral Upbringing (DE p. 129): 120 childhood XP in the wilderness Abilities and no Language. Covenant Upbringing
  (DE p. 124): childhood XP may buy Latin and Order of Hermes Lore.

Optimisation and technical debt
- The die renderer, specs, driver and packer now live in the toolkit (tools/dice), with a README entry; any die can be
  re-rendered and repacked with two commands.
- Start-up: the Virtue-list reconciliation key skips Unicode work for plain-ASCII names and caches; the list sorts
  compute each key once with one collator; the header switches measure on the next frame instead of forcing a
  layout mid-parse. Under the profiler, time to DOMContentLoaded fell from about 1.12 s to 1.05 s (median of three)
  and the switch measurement from about 98 ms to 69 ms; unprofiled wall time is within run-to-run noise.
