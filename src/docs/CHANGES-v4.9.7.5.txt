Build layout (no rule changes)
- Folded sections show a one-line summary of their contents (names, scores, chips) beside the section's status.
- Any section can be pinned to the side panel (up to four), shown there in full; "Pin another section" adds one.
- After a jump from the rail or a pinned card, "Back to <section>" returns to the section and place you left.
- The rail is grouped in four steps: Who (Concept, House, Characteristics), Gifts and flaws (Virtues and Flaws),
  Skills and magic (Abilities, Arts, Spells, Hedge Magic, Laboratory, Familiar), Gear and story (Combat, Inventory,
  Personality, Biography), plus Tools. Below 1240 px it is a row of four step chips.
- Laboratory, Familiar and Hedge Magic start folded and dimmed until started; their button reads "Start".
- Open all, Fold all, and "Fold the other sections when I open one" (on by default; kept in this browser).
- Gender, race or nationality, religion, place of origin, title, height, weight, hair, eyes and handedness move from
  Concept to the top of Biography ("Particulars"). The saved data is unchanged.
- Abilities: the life-stage pools are tabs with their budgets (a tick when spent), plus "All scores".
- Each section's help text sits behind an (i) button with its page references. A rule check that fails is shown at
  the top of its own section. Rules notes longer than about 240 characters fold to their first sentence.
- Side panel: wards in a 3 x 2 grid; rule checks start folded unless one fails; PDF sheet options moved to the
  Export PDF dialog.
- Header: Export PDF, Save .json and Load .json are in one File menu in both modes. On a phone the header is one row
  (seal, title, Build/Play, menu), and the theme, settings and credits are in that menu.
- Phones and tablets (980 px and below): a sticky status strip (Flaws, Virtues, experience left, spell levels,
  checks) replaces the side panel above the form; tapping it opens the side panel over the page.

Auras (Play › Modifiers)
- Dominion: one weaker at night, one stronger on Sundays and holy days (RoP:D p. 10; DE p. 418).
- Infernal: +1 at night, +1 under an Interdict, +1 Wednesday to Good Friday of Holy Week, -1 on a kept Shabbat,
  0 from Good Friday dusk to Easter Monday and on the Night of Destiny, -2 for the rest of Ramadan; never above 10
  (RoP:I pp. 10-11). Changing the strength by hand clears these.
- Aligned Magic auras (RoP:M pp. 15-16): an Art alignment adds the aura again to spells using that Art; Healing adds
  half the aura (rounded up) to Recovery; Luck offers +1/+2/+3 (aura 1-4/5-8/9-10) on each roll; Distracting raises
  Concentration Ease Factors by 3 at aura 5+; an emotion alignment gives +1/+2/+3 (aura 1-3/4-6/7-10).
- Infernal auras (RoP:I pp. 12-17): Recovery rolls take -aura. Debauched: Confidence cannot be spent. Malevolent: an
  optional +aura botch dice on physical Characteristic and Ability rolls, and attack rolls note +aura to Damage.
  Perfidious: noted (+aura to the Ease Factor to spot a lie). In an Infernal regio holy powers and holy magic take
  -aura. Tartaran and Abyssal regiones: in an Abyssal regio each Characteristic in a roll is -aura.
  True Faith 1+ is immune to the tarnish, corrupted and regio effects (p. 13).

Realms tab
- Regio sight (DE p. 421): Perception + Second Sight, Magic Sensitivity, or Sense Holiness and Unholiness (Divine and
  Infernal) + stress die vs 5 + 2 x the difference, or 5 + 2 x (current aura + level) from another realm's aura;
  lost near the boundary rolls with 0.
- Faerie regiones: Perception + Awareness with Faerie Sight, Second Sight or Magic Sensitivity (RoP:F p. 16; optional
  rule, on); guessing the way in, Intelligence + Faerie Lore vs 15, or 3 when the story narrows the choice (p. 16).
- Celestial disorientation (RoP:D pp. 14-15): Stamina + Dominion Lore (or Parma Magica if higher) + stress die vs
  rating x 3; failure gives -(rating x 3) to every roll until you leave; exposure limit Stamina + Parma + 3 hours.
- Corruption roll (RoP:I pp. 11-12): stress die + temporary Infernal aura vs 3 + strongest aura (maintain) and
  6 + strongest aura (increase); sacrifice bonus; Confidence for a corruption rite; erosion time of a neglected aura.
- Dominion rite (RoP:D pp. 11-12): Communication + Theology or Dominion Lore (2+) + stress die vs 6, with what it needs
  and how the Dominion grows and fades.
- Empyreal aura from a miracle, 1 at level 50 and +1 per 5 levels; a Divine creature's home up to Might / 10
  (RoP:D pp. 12-13). Faerie aura = Might / 5; two faeries clashing, 2 + the higher Might / 5, at most 9 (RoP:F p. 14).
- The covenant aura (RoP:M pp. 8-14): tethers (natural 1-2; residue, monument, being, burial and vis-source
  calculators), base aura = natural (at most 5) + preternatural (strongest + 1 per other, at most 5); the yearly aura
  modifier (held to +-9); stable years lower the aura's botch dice; the Strengthening and Weakening tables with botch
  dice +1 after each 9+; permanent changes and regio levels recorded; a log of years.

Vis
- Raw vis rows have a kind: ordinary; Faerie, Divine or Infernal (DE p. 422); vis infesta, sordida or prava
  (RoP:I pp. 18-19); dedicated or extraordinary (RoP:M pp. 119-123); encased, anchor, accrued, sleeping or abstract
  (RoP:F pp. 18-19). Each kind's rules are noted under the table.
- Spending vis in a formulaic or Ritual casting (Play › Modifiers): Infernal vis and vis infesta double the botch dice
  (DE p. 422; optional rule for The Infernal's reading); vis sordida +5 a pawn and three botch dice a pawn, never fewer
  than the pawns; vis prava makes the roll a zero with two botch dice a pawn; an infernally aligned caster avoids
  these; dedicated vis +4 a pawn and double botch dice for its own Technique and Form; abstract vis gives nothing.
- Post-Gauntlet laboratory: "Vis pawns used, by year" is checked against Magic Theory x 5 a year (DE p. 50).
- Optional rule (off): large stores of one Art's vis cause spontaneous spells (RoP:M p. 123).

Optional rules added (Settings › Optional rules)
- Sub: faerie regio sight with Awareness (on); vis infesta with one botch die a pawn (off); stored vis (off).
- Homebrew: count every magical botch in the yearly aura modifier (off).

Not changed

- Start-up and mode-switch speed on phones were not changed in this release.
