Hedge magic: known effects finished (Hedge Magic, Revised Edition)
- Folk witches: Known Effects lists the published effects with their Ease Factors: the eight curses (Disease, Miscarriage,
  Aging, Emotion, Tongue, Paralysis, Beast, Reduction; pp. 37–38), Flight (p. 38), and the Healing and Animal Healing
  table of wounds and Fatigue levels with their vis costs (pp. 35, 38). Custom entries take a power, Ease Factor and method.
- Elementalists: entries record Technique, elemental Form and effect level, with the summoning chart (pp. 23–26).
  HMRE prints no named elementalist effects, so none are offered from the book.
- Each known effect loads its Play roll with the power and Ease Factor, or Technique, Form and level, already set.
- Nightwalkers: unchanged; their powers have fixed rolls, all already in Play (pp. 106–108).

True Faith and the Divine (Realms of Power: The Divine; Definitive Edition)
- True Faith Score (1 per True Faith Virtue taken, or set in Play), Faith Points with a dawn refresh, and a test or
  crisis of faith that stops spending (RoP:D pp. 57–60; DE p. 419).
- Magic Resistance of True Faith Score × 10. It does not stack with other Magic Resistance; the higher total applies
  (DE p. 55).
- Faith Points spent on a roll like Confidence: +3 each, up to the True Faith Score per roll (one at a time without a
  score); +6 each from True Faith 3 (Hope).
- Embodying the Light: Devotion (2: Premonitions, dispel illusions and glamours with a Faith Point), Hope (3: +6 per
  Faith Point, Magic Resistance doubled against Mentem), Charity (5: an Empyreal aura of score – 4 at Voice range)
  (RoP:D pp. 61–62).
- Pray for a miracle: stress die + True Faith + Concentration (or a Holy Method) + aura against the miracle's level,
  adjusted for timeliness (+10 to –20) and need (–15 to +20); extra Faith Points +3 each; group prayer with Ceremony;
  Faith Points spent from the result; botch and Warping notes (RoP:D pp. 60–62).
- Invoke God's aid: stress die + Communication + Dominion Lore, at least three botch dice on a zero; 9+/15+/24+ give
  +1/+2/+3 to the scene's rolls that further the objective, ending automatically at the first failed roll; group
  prayer; paid with a Confidence or Faith Point (RoP:D pp. 41–42).

Tempers and tarnishes
- Temper a Dominion aura: stress die + Presence + Leadership (+ Ceremony when ceremonial; Uncertain Faith –3),
  9+/15+/24+ for +1/+2/+3, with the seven tempers and their virtues and vices (RoP:D pp. 38–40).
- Tarnish an Infernal aura: stress die + Presence + Leadership, 9+ to 24+ for 1 to 6 (RoP:I pp. 23–24).
- An active temper offers +value / –value on rolls in the Divine aura; an active tarnish offers +value / –value on
  Personality rolls in the Infernal aura (none with True Faith, RoP:I p. 13). Each fades by a day, morning or season.
  Aura modifiers set a temper or tarnish laid by someone else.

Ars Notoria (RoP:D pp. 92, 97–100)
- Recitation total = Intelligence + Ars Notoria + modifiers (fasting, chastity, honorable and dishonorable Flaws,
  sins, non-Divine Supernatural Abilities, True Faith, storyguide) + stress die; Ease Factor 6 (first) or 3 (known)
  + 3 × ring + 1 per season. Rings are first learned in order.
- Active rings in Play: 100 XP in Philosophiae, Theology or Medicine; Communication or Intelligence +3 to a maximum of
  +5; the fifth ring's realm sensing; the sixth ring's divination; the seventh ring's stress dice without botches.
- Rolls for the search for the Book of Solomon (Intelligence + Dominion Lore vs 21) and for fasting (Stamina vs 6, +1
  per consecutive season).
- The Ars Notoria score reduces the Divine aura penalty to other magic by 3 per point.
- Build: checks Artes Liberales, Latin, Greek, Hebrew and Chaldean at 4, and the creation eligibility; adds the 50 XP
  pool for the languages. Ars Notoria is now a Supernatural Ability (it was filed as Academic).
