Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Source verification v4.9.3.5
Date: 2026-10-26

Scope
The v4.9.3.1 audit recovered Range, Duration and Target for 65 spells. In 39 of them the Target field also held the first
line of the spell's description (for example "Ind The spell removes all pain not exceeding the pain of a light wound from t").

Method
For each affected spell the source book's text was searched for the printed "R: …, D: …, T: …" line that is followed by
that description. The match was accepted only when the Range and Duration agree with the record and the Technique/Form and
level printed immediately before the line agree with the record. 38 matched in this way; Eternal Repetition in a Bottomless
Pool (Covenants p. 101) was read directly from the page, where the layout splits its Target onto the line above.
Remain Within Those Walls (Magi of Hermes p. 101) prints "T: Arcane Circle"; the record had "Arcane".
Two older records (Hinder the Gift, Recognize Your Master) held their base-level breakdown in Target; it was moved to the
empty Base field.
