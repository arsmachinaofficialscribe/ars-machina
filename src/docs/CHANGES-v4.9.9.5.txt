Version v4.9.9.5 (Realms in realm sub-tabs)
Build date: 2026-10-28

Scope: a layout and loading change to Play › Realms. No rule values changed. Save format unchanged; two optional

Play › Realms
- Sub-tabs: Divine, Infernal, Faerie, Magic, Places. Only the chosen sub-tab's sections are drawn; each sub-tab shows
  how many tools it holds. The section strip above the sections is not shown on this tab.
  Divine: holy magic, holy powers, True Faith, miracles, God's aid, tempers and tarnishes, relics, saints, the Divine
  Ascent, Ars Notoria, golems, Dominion and Empyreal auras. Infernal: maleficia, Ars Goetia, sin and temptation,
  Confidence through sin, Infernal influence, the infernalist, Infernal auras. Faerie: faerie wizardry, Faerie auras,
  the Faerie Realm. Magic: the covenant aura, a magic character's year, Things of Virtue and warped beasts, tamed
  creatures. Places: regiones, Warping by realm. Tempers and tarnishes sits under Infernal when only the infernal side
  applies.
- Gating by the sheet (our reading; the books do not limit these tools to particular characters):
  God's aid, saints, Dominion auras: True Faith or Faith Points, Dominion Lore, a holy Power or Method, Holy Magic,
  a clergy Virtue, Pious, Sense Holiness and Unholiness, or a Divine being (a chosen saint or a Divine aura also keeps
  them).
  Tempers and tarnishes: as above, or Infernal Lore, spiritual authority, or a temper or tarnish in force.
  Sin and temptation: a Personality Trait or Flaw tied to a sin or virtue, a sin recorded, or Infernal Lore / an
  infernalist / an Infernal being. Confidence through sin: a trait with a penchant for a sin (RoP:I p. 23).
  Infernal influence: that, or Infernal Lore. Infernal auras: Infernal Lore, an infernalist, an Infernal being, or an
  Infernal aura.
  The Faerie Realm and Faerie auras: Faerie Lore, a Virtue or Flaw naming faeries or Arcadia, a Faerie being, a visit
  in progress or fable points (or a Faerie aura).
  The covenant aura: a magus, Magic Lore, a Magic being or a Magic aura. Things of Virtue: Magic Lore, a Magic being
  or a Thing of Virtue recorded. Tamed creatures: Animal Handling, Animal Ken or a creature on the list.
  Regiones: a magus, any realm Lore, or Second Sight. Warping by realm: Warping Points or a recorded source.
  Sections that were already drawn only for a character who has them (True Faith, holy magic and powers, maleficia,
  Ars Goetia, faerie wizardry, Ars Notoria, the infernalist, the Divine Ascent, relics, golems, a magic character's
  year) keep their own tests. Books switched off still remove their sections.
- Show all tools: a switch that draws every section of the chosen sub-tab; its label counts the hidden ones.
- Descriptions: each section's description, and any long plain note in its body, sits behind an "i" button beside the
  section title (aria-expanded, aria-controls). Open descriptions stay open across redraws for the session.
- Overflow: inside this tab buttons wrap their labels, fields and action grids shrink to the card, tables scroll
  inside their card, and cards are tighter (padding 10px 12px, gaps 8px 14px).
