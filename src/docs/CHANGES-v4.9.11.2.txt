Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.11.2 (Roll screen hotfix)
Build date: 2026-11-09
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: three fixes to the roll ceremony reported against 4.9.11.1. No rule or content change; save formats unchanged.

- No automatic close. The result stage scheduled fxClose after 1.9 s (1.6 s with reduced motion, +0.8 s when
  skipped). That timer is removed (one counted replacement). The screen closes on a click, a tap, a key, or its
  existing controls. This was the behaviour before 4.9.11.1 too; it is changed at Haik's request.
- Retro roll circle reverted. In Retro, fxCircleSvg0 returns the pre-4.9.11.1 drawing again, and the pixel
  renderer's layer list for the circle is back to fx-r1..fx-r4. Reasons: the 4.9.11.1 medallions rendered as filled
  studs that ignored the Art tint (the renderer copies fill, and the tint rules set stroke), they were cloned into
  the Imaginem ghost layers, and the renderer forces opacity to 1 so nothing faded in. The circle's SVG is now
  cached per skin (fxCircleSvg replaced), so switching skin takes effect on the next roll.
- Modern roll circle: medallions (g.fx-med) fade in after 0.5 s instead of showing their dark fill at once; the
  wood, iron and Characteristic dice tints and the botch tint reach the icons and rim lozenges; medallions and
  lozenges are hidden inside .fx-ghost copies.
- The seal and the Play crest are unchanged from 4.9.11.1 in both skins.
