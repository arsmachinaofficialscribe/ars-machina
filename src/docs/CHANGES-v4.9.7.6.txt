Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.7.6 (Build grouping; upright portraits)
Build date: 2026-10-27
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Build sheet
- The sections stand in the rail's order: Who (Concept, House, Characteristics); Gifts and Flaws (Virtues and Flaws);
  Skills and Magic (Abilities, Arts, Spells, Might-Based Being, Hedge Magic, Laboratory, Familiar); Gear and Story
  (Combat, Inventory, Personality, Biography); Tools (Calculator). A divider with the step's name stands before each
  group. Characteristics now come before Virtues and Flaws on the sheet.
- Seals are numbered in sheet order; the rail's seals match. (Hedge Magic no longer shares a seal with Spells.)

Sections that need a Virtue or a character type
- Hedge Magic: Known Effects is shown only with a hedge tradition's Virtue: Folk Witch (HMRE p. 34), Gruagach
  (p. 56), Gruagach Master (p. 57), Mathematicus of Bologna (p. 80), Vitki (p. 122), Elementalist Air, Earth, Fire
  or Water (p. 18), (Type) Controlling, Divining or Refining (p. 19), (Type) Summoning (p. 20), Elemental Medicine,
  Philosophiae or Theurgy (p. 19), or Elemental Magic taken by a non-magus.
  Change: a character with only Second Sight (or another folk power) and no Folk Witch Virtue no longer shows the
  folk witch block. Effects already recorded stay in the file and return when the Virtue is taken.
- Arts, Spells, Laboratory and Familiar (magi only), and Hedge Magic without its Virtue, are not on the sheet; the
  rail shows them greyed and not selectable, with the reason in a tip.
- The Play tab is unchanged.

Headings and type
- The app's headings are in Title Case (minor words lower case except first, last and after a colon; abbreviations
  such as "yr" and "pp." kept). Character names and other entered text are not changed. Buttons stay in sentence case.
- The rail and the rule checks share one typeset: panel headings 12 px small caps; group names 13 px small caps;
  entries 14 px; counts and states 12.5 px.

Portraits
- Upright frame, 100 x 130 (window 82 x 112), in the Build slot, the Play header, the crop dialog (300 x 390), the
  character card (158 x 205 niche) and the PDF sheet (112 x 146 pt).
- Stored picture 540 x 702. Transparent portraits are kept as PNG through cropping, pixelization and re-cutting.
- Pixelization works at the picture's own size (blocks unchanged).
- Square portraits in older files are re-cut on load: from the original picture and its crop when the file has one,
  otherwise by trimming the sides; a pixelized portrait is re-cut from its unpixelized copy and pixelized again.
- Play header: the portrait is 200 px wide (164 px at 980 px and below, 132 px at 700, 112 px at 560).

Not changed
- No rule values changed. Save format unchanged.
