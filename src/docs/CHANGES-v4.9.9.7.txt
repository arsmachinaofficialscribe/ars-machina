Ars Machina — Scribe and Cast
Version v4.9.9.7 (Play screen tidy)
Build date: 2026-10-28
Package: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload) and a self-contained offline HTML file

Scope: layout and persistence; no rule values changed. Numbered as a patch at the author's request. Save format
unchanged; new optional play fields x.rlmOn (per-tool choices) and fight ({on, round}) are read with coercion only.

Play › Realms
- "Show all tools" is replaced by "Tools shown: n of m", a fold listing every Realms tool available to the sheet,
  grouped Divine, Infernal, Faerie, Magic and Places. Each tool can be ticked or unticked; tools the sheet does not
  call for are marked "not on the sheet". "Show all" ticks everything; "Only what the sheet uses" clears the choices.
  Choices are saved with the sheet per tool title. Sections a switched-off book removes are not listed.
- The empty-tab note points to Situation for the aura and to Tools shown.

Play › Situation
- Calm or Under stress, Aura, Mounted and Exertion form one framed strip of 36 px cells that share the width; the
  aura cell keeps room for its realm (the chosen realm stays readable from 390 to 1440 px, measured), and below
  620 px the strip stacks. "Fatigue and wounds apply on their own" moves to the Situation label's tooltip.

Play › every tab
- A section's description longer than 90 characters, and plain body notes longer than 140 characters (no bold,
  links or controls), sit behind the "i" button beside the section title, as on Realms since 4.9.9.5. Open
  descriptions stay open across redraws for the session. Notes carrying figures in bold stay in place.

Persistence
- A fight in progress and its round were lost on reload and on switching characters; they are now kept per sheet
  in play.fight and restored.
